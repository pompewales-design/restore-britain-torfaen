Restore Britain Torfaen

The Restore Britain Torfaen branch provides a local focus for people interested in the issues affecting our communities.

The branch aims to understand what is happening locally, examine matters of public interest and provide information about issues affecting people across Torfaen.

We are interested in evidence, accountability and constructive discussion about the future of our communities.

This website brings together local information, policy papers, Freedom of Information work, news and resources relating to the Torfaen branch.

Getting involved

You do not have to be a member to raise a local concern or suggest something that may deserve further investigation. Use the contact page or the local issue facility on the homepage to get in touch with the branch.
